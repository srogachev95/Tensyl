Geometry and Fields API

Geometry surfaces for stiffness embedding.

SurfacePoint dataclass

Differential geometry data at a parametric surface point.

Attributes:
  • u (float) –

    First parametric coordinate after validation.

  • v (float) –

    Second parametric coordinate after validation.

  • position (FloatArray) –

    Three-dimensional point on the midsurface.

  • tangent_u (FloatArray) –

    Physical tangent vector for increasing u.

  • tangent_v (FloatArray) –

    Physical tangent vector for increasing v.

  • metric (FloatArray) –

    First fundamental form in the (u, v) chart.

  • curvature (FloatArray) –

    Second fundamental form in the (u, v) chart.

  • frame (Frame2D) –

    Right-handed local tangent frame used by stiffness values.

  • jacobian (float) –

    Positive surface area scale for the parameterization.

  • principal_curvatures (tuple[float, float]) –

    The two signed principal curvatures.

  • min_radius (float) –

    Smallest positive curvature radius, or infinity for flat directions.

  • metadata (Mapping[str, Any]) –

    Read-only provenance for the surface and coordinate names.

Cylinder dataclass

Circular cylinder parameterized by axial coordinate and angle (x, theta).

The local frame has e1 in the axial direction, e2 in the circumferential direction, and n outward.

Attributes:
  • radius (float) –

    Positive cylinder radius.

  • length (float | None) –

    Optional positive axial length used as model metadata by callers. The parameterization itself does not clip u.

  • label (str) –

    Frame and metadata label attached to sampled points.

ConicalFrustum dataclass

Circular conical frustum parameterized by axial coordinate and angle.

Coordinates are (x, theta). The local frame uses e1 along increasing axial station/generator direction, e2 opposite the positive theta tangent, and n outward. The apex is not part of the supported smooth midsurface domain.

Attributes:
  • radius_start (float) –

    Positive radius at u = 0.

  • radius_end (float) –

    Positive radius at u = length.

  • length (float) –

    Positive axial length.

  • label (str) –

    Frame and metadata label attached to sampled points.

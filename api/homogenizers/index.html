Homogenizers API

Tangent-plane equivalent-stiffness homogenizers.

HomogenizationFailure

Bases: Exception

Base exception for homogenization failures.

Use this when callers want to catch all Tensyl homogenization errors without also catching unrelated ValueError instances.

HomogenizationInputError

Bases: HomogenizationFailure, ValueError

Raised when a homogenizer receives malformed or unsupported input.

HomogenizationNumericalError

Bases: HomogenizationFailure, ArithmeticError

Raised when an assembled tangent violates the homogenizer's numeric contract.

ValidityContext dataclass

Optional geometric scale data for tangent-plane validity checks.

characteristic_height is a stiffness or stiffener height scale, pitch is the repeated-cell spacing, min_radius is the smallest local curvature radius, and response_length is the intended structural response length such as a buckle wavelength or analysis feature size.

Attributes:
  • characteristic_height (float | None) –

    Optional positive member or wall height scale.

  • pitch (float | None) –

    Optional positive repeated-cell pitch.

  • min_radius (float | None) –

    Optional positive curvature radius, or infinity for flat geometry.

  • response_length (float | None) –

    Optional positive structural response length.

The stiffness is returned with validity attached so warnings remain available when only result.stiffness is passed to fields, exports, or downstream tools.

Forces are per member, independent of its represented length/multiplicity. They are conjugate to the five member strains documented by member_loads.

  • transverse_shear_force (float) –

member_loads(cell, eta)

Recover first-approximation member forces and moments from wall strain.

In member axes the five strains are eps11 + zakappa11, (gamma12 + zskappa12)/2, gamma13, kappa11, and -kappa12/2. Multiplying by EA, kGAy, kGAz, EIy, and GJ gives the conjugate loads. Omitted shear stiffnesses give zero shear force, as in homogenization.

Parameters:
  • cell (CanonicalUnitCell) –

    Cell whose members and convention define the recovery model.

  • eta (GeneralizedStrain | FloatArray) –

    Finite generalized strain in the cell frame, shape (8,).
Returns:
  • MemberLoads –

    Loads in cell.members order, per physical member. These are the

  • ... –

    affine model's beam resultants, not local stresses or joint loads.
Raises:
  • HomogenizationInputError –

    If the cell convention is unsupported.

  • ValueError –

    If strains or recovered loads are invalid or non-finite.

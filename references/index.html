References

These sources supply the plate, laminate, section, and homogenization mechanics used throughout the handbook. Equations and source comparisons link to the relevant entry or primary publication.

Equivalent-Plate and Stiffened-Wall Theory

  • Michael P. Nemeth, A Treatise on Equivalent-Plate Stiffnesses for Stiffened Laminated-Composite Plates and Plate-Like Lattices, NASA/TP-2011-216882, NASA Langley Research Center, 2011. External record: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20110004039.
  • Michael P. Nemeth, An In-Depth Tutorial on Constitutive Equations for Elastic Anisotropic Materials, NASA/TM-2011-217314, 2011. External record: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20110023650.

Section Properties

  • MIT OpenCourseWare, 16.20 Structural Mechanics, Fall 2002, torsion notes. Thin-wall open and closed-section torsion, including Bredt shear flow.

  • R. C. Hibbeler, Statics and Mechanics of Materials, 2nd ed., Pearson Prentice Hall, 2004.

Plates, Shells, and Laminates

  • Alan T. Nettles, Basic Mechanics of Laminated Composite Plates, NASA RP-1351,

  • NASA record. Laminate stiffness and compliance, engineering constants, and thermal resultants.

  • Manfredo P. do Carmo, Differential Geometry of Curves and Surfaces, Prentice-Hall, 1976.

  • Eric Reissner, "The Effect of Transverse Shear Deformation on the Bending of Elastic Plates", Journal of Applied Mechanics, 1945.
  • R. D. Mindlin, "Influence of Rotatory Inertia and Shear on Flexural Motions of Isotropic, Elastic Plates", Journal of Applied Mechanics, 1951.
  • J. N. Reddy, Mechanics of Laminated Composite Plates and Shells: Theory and Analysis, 2nd ed., CRC Press, 2004.
  • Robert M. Jones, Mechanics of Composite Materials, Hemisphere Publishing, 1975.

Shell Buckling Context

  • Mark W. Hilburger, Buckling of Thin-Walled Circular Cylinders, NASA/SP-8007-2020/REV 2, NASA Langley Research Center, 2020. External record: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20205011530.

Homogenization Context

  • Alain Bensoussan, Jacques-Louis Lions, and George Papanicolaou, Asymptotic Analysis for Periodic Structures, North-Holland, 1978.
  • E. Sanchez-Palencia, Non-Homogeneous Media and Vibration Theory, Springer-Verlag, 1980.
  • N. S. Bakhvalov and G. P. Panasenko, Homogenisation: Averaging Processes in Periodic Media, Kluwer Academic Publishers, 1989.
